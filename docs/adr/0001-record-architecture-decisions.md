# ADR 0001: Record architecture decisions

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-08-09

## Context
This build exists to demonstrate judgement, not just working code. The reasoning
behind a design is the part that transfers to an internal platform review; the
code is the part that gets rewritten.

## Decision
Every non-obvious structural choice gets an ADR: context, decision, consequences,
and what would make us revisit it. Written when the decision is made, not
reconstructed afterwards.

## Consequences
- Design reviews start from a written position rather than a whiteboard.
- Reversal is cheap and explicit: supersede the ADR, keep the original.
- Costs roughly fifteen minutes per decision.
